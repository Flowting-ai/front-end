Project & Chat Sharing Model

How visibility, ownership, and deletion work for the two entities that get shared: projects and chats.

00Entities

Two things get shared in Souvenir: projects, which hold work and structure, and chats, which live inside or outside a project. Both follow the same underlying principle — one owner per entity, and that owner is never removable by anyone but themselves.

Two roles exist: Member and Admin. Admins carry no special sharing power in this model — the only Admin-specific rule anywhere is that the last Admin in a workspace can't leave without promoting a replacement first, which is a workspace safeguard, not a sharing rule.

Ownership & membership

  • Every project has exactly one owner, fixed at creation, holding the only role with power to delete the project or resolve its succession.
  • Everyone else is a collaborator — no deletion rights, no successorship, but free to add or remove other collaborators.
  • The owner cannot be removed by anyone else, under any circumstance. They can only exit voluntarily, and even that is gated (below).
  • On a Shared project, any collaborator can add or remove any other collaborator — the owner is the one exception nobody can touch.
  • Workspace projects have no manageable member list — access is simply "everyone currently in the workspace."

Leaving & reassignment

Collaborators can leave a Shared or Workspace project at any time, no confirmation needed. Owners cannot — a project can never be left ownerless, so exiting is a deliberate handoff, not a departure.

02Chats

States

A chat has three possible states: Private, Shared, or Archived. There's no "leaving" a chat — that concept doesn't exist here the way it does for projects.

Sharing model

  • Inside a project: a Shared chat is automatically visible to every member of that project and listed on the project's home-page. Its audience is inherited from the project — there's no separate list to manage.
  • Outside a project: a Shared chat has its own explicit list, same shape as a Shared project's — but unlike projects, only the creator can add or remove people from it. No open-collaborator editing here, since chats have no collaborator concept at all.
  • A chat's project association is fixed at creation and can't change — it can't move between projects or move into one later.
  • Once shared, the creator becomes the chat's owner in the same sticky sense as a project owner: nobody else can remove them, and there's no successor mechanism — chats don't get handed off.

Viewing & continuing

Anyone a chat is shared with can view it in full, but can't edit or add to it. To keep the conversation going, they hit Continue, which forks a private copy into their own account. The original stays exactly as the creator left it.

03Edge cases

Ownership & exit

Can an owner be removed by an Admin, or anyone else?

No, under any circumstance. The only way ownership changes hands is the owner voluntarily naming a successor.

What if the owner closes the successor/archive modal without choosing?

They stay stuck as owner, still in the project, unable to complete the exit. No timeout, no fallback path.

Multiple people are eligible successors — how does the system pick?

It doesn't. The outgoing owner picks manually. There's no tiebreaker because there's no auto-assignment left in the model.

The last Admin tries to leave the workspace — what happens?

Same pattern as project ownership: a forced modal requires promoting a replacement Admin before the leave completes.

Membership

Who can add or remove people from a Shared project?

Any collaborator — fully open. The one person nobody can remove is the owner.

A non-owner is removed from a Shared project — what happens to their access?

Immediate and total, including any chat history they could previously see in that project.

Can a non-owner just leave a Shared or Workspace project on their own?

Yes, self-serve, no confirmation. This only applies to collaborators — owners are gated as described above.

Offboarding

Someone is offboarded from the workspace entirely — what happens to what they own?

Personal projects are deleted. Any project they own as Shared/Workspace owner must be resolved (successor named, or archived/converted if they're alone) before the offboarding can complete — same gate as a manual exit.

What happens to their Private chats?

Deleted, permanently, with no recovery — same treatment as Personal projects.

What happens to chats they'd already shared?

They survive. Visibility for a Shared chat lives with its list or its project, not with the creator's continued presence in the workspace.

Deletion

Can a collaborator ever delete a project?

No. Deletion is owner-only across all three project types, regardless of how the current owner came to hold that role.

What happens to the chats inside a deleted project?

Deleted along with it — Private and Shared alike. Nothing is orphaned back out to individual owners.

Is deletion immediate?

Soft-delete with a 30-day recovery window, then an automatic purge. Applies to projects; chat deletion is instant with no recovery window.

Chats

Can a Private chat exist inside a Workspace or Shared project?

Yes — chat visibility is independent of the project it lives in. A Private chat stays creator-only even inside a fully Shared project, and won't appear on the project's home-page.

Can a viewer edit a chat that's been shared with them?

No. Shared chats are view-only. To keep chatting, a viewer forks their own copy via Continue.

Can a chat move between projects, or into one after creation?

No. Its project association is fixed at creation, same as its sharing state.

Is a Shared chat searchable workspace-wide?

No. It's discoverable only via its project's home-page (if inside a project) or directly by the people on its list (if standalone) — never through general workspace search.
